Privacy Policy

  1. Who we are. MateBot AI is provided by Matebot AI Pty Ltd and is used by Apartment Living Melbourne (ALM) to handle housing enquiries.
  2. What we collect. The details you give in the MateBot or HomeBot enquiry form: your name and contact details, the dates of your stay, budget, room and apartment preferences, where you will study or work, how you prefer to travel and for how long, the people enquiring with you, your household habits and preferences, and your introduction. The MateBot form also asks your age (optional), sex, country of birth, the country and city you live in now and the languages you speak. These are used only to introduce you to the people in a proposed group; they are not used to decide who is matched with whom or to rank anyone. ALM staff may add notes about your enquiry.
  3. How we use it. To match you with housemates, to find suitable whole apartments, and so the ALM team can contact you about your enquiry. With the agreement you give in the form, your name, contact details and introduction may be shared with the people in your proposed group.
  4. Use of AI. Parts of your enquiry are processed by an AI service (Claude, provided by Anthropic) to help ALM staff assess possible groups and apartment options and to draft introductions. ALM staff review AI results and send any introduction themselves; the AI does not send messages to you by itself.
  5. Automatic messages. Automatic sending is disabled during the current controlled pilot. When enabled for normal service, two kinds of message are sent automatically and do not involve the AI: a fixed confirmation email to you when your enquiry is received, and internal alerts to the ALM reservations team when an enquiry needs attention.
  6. Journey estimates. To estimate journey times, four things are sent to Google Maps: the address of a proposed apartment building, the address of your place of study or work, the way of travelling (for example tram or walking) and the departure time used for the estimate. No name, email address or phone number field is sent. If the place of work you gave is itself a personal or identifying address, that address is what is sent. Journey estimates are shown to ALM staff when they look at your options and are not kept by MateBot AI. Google's handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  7. Where it is kept. Enquiry records are kept in MateBot AI's database, hosted with DigitalOcean in its Sydney region. This describes where the database is hosted. It does not describe where the AI service (Anthropic) or Google Maps process the information sent to them; see their own policies.
  8. Cookies and browser storage. Staff sign-in sets one essential cookie (named __Host-mmai_session) that keeps a staff member signed in; it can be read only by this site, is sent only over a secure connection, lasts up to 30 days and is removed on sign-out. The MateBot enquiry form keeps one random reference in your browser for the current tab (session storage) so that pressing submit twice cannot create two enquiries; it holds none of your answers and is gone when the tab closes. If you choose "Save and finish later", your answers so far are saved on our server and you are given a private link to return to them; a saved draft is deleted after 12 months. The network that delivers the site may set its own short-lived security cookie. No advertising or analytics cookies are used.
  9. How long we keep it. If you do not go on to lease, your details are kept for 12 months from our last contact with you and are then deleted.
  10. Access, correction, deletion and complaints. To ask what details we hold about you, to have them corrected or deleted, or to make a complaint about how your details have been handled, email reservations@almelb.com.au. We will reply and tell you what we have done.
  11. Changes. This policy may be updated. Last updated: 16 November 2026.
