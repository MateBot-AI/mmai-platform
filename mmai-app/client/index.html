Meet your perfect housemates.

MateBot AI is Apartment Living Melbourne's AI-powered matchmaking program, built to find you housemates you'll genuinely click with. Tell us about you: your study or work, your dates, your budget, your lifestyle and the kind of people you'd love to live with.

MateBot AI runs a deep compatibility analysis of your answers against everyone else moving to Melbourne at the same time, then puts together a highly compatible group. MateBot then hand-picks the fully furnished ALM apartments that best suit your group, close to where you'll be studying or working.

When we've found your match, we introduce you to each other so you can chat, meet and move in together. It takes a couple of minutes.
