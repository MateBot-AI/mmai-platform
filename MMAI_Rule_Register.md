## Section G: Governance of this register

**G1. Constitution.** This register is the constitution and foundation of MateBot AI. Nothing may be built, written or changed for MateBot AI that is inconsistent with a rule in this register.

**G2. Mandatory reference.** The register must be consulted before starting, and checked again before delivering, any MateBot AI software, document or change, no matter how small or how far in the future.

**G3. Recording new rules.** Everything new that Bill and the assistant agree on for MateBot AI is added to this register as soon as it is agreed.

**G4. One rule only.** When a new ruling supersedes an existing rule, the existing rule is deleted and the new rule takes its place. Superseded rules are not kept alongside current rules.

**G5. Contradictions require Bill's approval.** If a proposed new ruling contradicts an existing rule, the assistant must raise the conflict with Bill and get his explicit approval before deleting and replacing the existing rule.

**G6. Only agreed items become rules.** Only instructions given or approved by Bill become rules. Assistant suggestions stay out of the register until Bill approves them.

**G7. Permanence.** A rule stays in force indefinitely, including for work done years from now, unless it is superseded under G4 and G5.

## Section B: ALM business model

**B1. Whole-apartment leasing only.** ALM leases whole apartments. ALM does not rent by the bed or by the room.

**B2. Nature of enquiries.** Most enquiries come from individuals responding to ALM's advertising campaigns. They enquire about either a single room in an ALM apartment or a single bed in a twin-share room in an ALM apartment.

**B3. Non-smoking apartments.** Smoking is not allowed inside any ALM apartment. Residents may smoke outside only, on a balcony or in a courtyard.

**B4. No pets.** Pets are never allowed in ALM apartments. The questionnaire has no pet question.

**B5. One whole lease, jointly and severally liable.** ALM accepts a lease only for the entire apartment, as one whole lease. Everyone named on the lease is jointly and severally liable for the full weekly rent (for example, $1,000 per week). How the tenants divide the rent among themselves is their own arrangement; ALM only requires the full weekly rent. If the rent is short, all the tenants receive the notice to vacate.

## Section P: Purpose of MateBot AI

**P1. Core function.** MateBot AI is ALM's matching software. It turns individual enquiries into whole-apartment tenancies by proposing groups of compatible individuals who together lease one ALM apartment.

**P2. Matching basis.** MateBot AI scores each individual enquiry against the live pool of enquiries and ALM's available apartments.

**P3. Group size.** Proposed groups have two to seven people and must stay within the apartment's bedding capacity under A4.

**P4. Model to replicate.** MateBot AI replicates the matchmaking that already happens in (a) Facebook groups, for example "Swedish students in Melbourne", where students arriving at the same time find each other and agree to rent together; and (b) the Flatmates platform's housemate-group section, where one person seeks a type of housemate and others respond and join until the group is complete.
